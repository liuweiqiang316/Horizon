### [Tencent reportedly leases Oracle AI chips](https://www.ft.com/content/8799b33d-f07c-4a03-82f0-bf5d3d1d29e9) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tencent reportedly signed a five-year lease with Oracle worth about $7 billion for roughly 100,000 advanced AI chips that it cannot buy directly in China. The deal is described as Tencent’s largest-ever overseas leasing transaction and would cover multiple data centers in Southeast Asia. The reported purpose is to accelerate development of Tencent’s AI models and agent tools. The source says U.S. rules bar Chinese companies from directly purchasing advanced chips but allow overseas leasing, with about 30% of the payment required upfront.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 1, 05:07

**「Background」** Advanced AI chips such as data-center GPUs are a key bottleneck for training and serving large AI models, and Chinese companies face U.S. export controls that restrict direct access to some high-end hardware. The reported Tencent-Oracle arrangement fits a broader workaround in which Chinese firms access computing capacity through overseas data centers rather than buying restricted chips for use in China.

**「Impact」** Tencent would gain overseas access to large-scale advanced AI compute for model and agent development despite restrictions on directly buying such chips in China, while Oracle data centers in Southeast Asia would become a key part of that capacity.
