### [LineageOS 24 Announced](https://lineageos.org/Changelog-32/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

LineageOS 24 was announced in a new project changelog, marking a major release of the open source Android-based operating system. The supplied discussion emphasizes continued support for many older devices, including the Pixel 4a, which matters for users trying to extend phone lifetimes and avoid hardware replacement. Commenters also highlighted an experimental generic bare-metal target credited in the excerpt to contributor 0xCAFEBABE, with reported successful boots on common x86\_64 PCs, Apple Silicon Macs, NVIDIA DGX Spark, and Qualcomm Snapdragon X Series laptops. The available evidence does not include the full changelog, so detailed upgrade requirements, Android base version, and official device list changes cannot be verified here.

hackernews · timschumi · Oct 11, 06:18 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50040276)

**「Background」** LineageOS is a free, open-source operating system based on Android for phones, tablets, and set-top boxes. It is community-developed and began as the successor to CyanogenMod after that project was forked in December 2016.

**「Impact」** LineageOS users and device maintainers gain another major release path, while the experimental generic target could broaden testing beyond traditional Android phones if it matures.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters were broadly positive about LineageOS preserving older hardware and about the possibility of running Android on more bare-metal systems. Concerns centered on discoverability of currently buyable supported devices and whether camera quality on custom ROMs still lags stock firmware on some phones.
