### [Buckmaster Statement Raises Questions About Navier–Stokes Research and AI](https://cims.nyu.edu/~tristanb/statement.pdf) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tristan Buckmaster’s personal statement describes claimed progress with Levent Alpöge on finite-time blowup problems involving incompressible porous media, the Boussinesq equations, and three-dimensional incompressible Euler flow. The work is not presented as a solution to the $1,000,000 Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize problem, although the authors reportedly believe a related result could inform that problem. The statement also alleges a dispute involving OpenAI over access to, use of, and attribution for mathematical work, making independent proof verification and the provenance of AI-assisted research central unresolved issues. Because the supplied item is a personal PDF without independent technical validation, its mathematical and factual claims remain unconfirmed.

hackernews · procedurecall · Sep 8, 05:42 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49605915)

**「Technical context」** The Navier–Stokes equations describe fluid motion, and a finite-time blowup result would show that a solution to a specified forced system becomes singular in finite time. This is related to, but distinct from, the Clay Millennium Prize problem, which asks whether smooth solutions to the three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes equations always exist and remain smooth.

**「Impact」** The episode highlights that researchers and AI companies may need clearer records of data use, authorship, attribution, and independent verification when AI systems are involved in advanced mathematical research.

**「Community Discussion」** Comments largely focused on the allegations that OpenAI may have benefited from de-identified user data and may have pressured researchers, while noting that the available account does not establish whether their work was used to train models. Other commenters summarized the claimed results as important but distinct from solving the Millennium Prize problem, and reactions were strongly critical of the alleged conduct despite the lack of independently verified evidence in the supplied material.
