### [xAI Announces Grok 4.7](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-7) ⭐️ 8.0/10

xAI announced Grok 4.7, a new version of its Grok frontier AI model, according to the submitted item. The supplied material does not include the official release text or verified technical specifications, so concrete claims about architecture, benchmarks, pricing, availability, and compatibility cannot be confirmed here. The release matters because it enters an increasingly competitive frontier-model market where developers are comparing Grok against models such as Opus and other high-end coding and agentic workflow systems. Discussion around the launch focused on whether Grok 4.7 represents a meaningful quality improvement over Grok 4.6 or mainly a higher-cost, slower incremental update.

hackernews · meetpateltech · Sep 21, 15:50 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49788838)

**「Background」** Grok is xAI’s large language model family, offered through consumer products and developer-facing APIs. Frontier AI model releases are commonly compared on reasoning and coding benchmarks, latency, and per-token API pricing; external reports say Grok 4.7 keeps Grok 4.6’s base API rates of $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens.

**「Impact」** Developers evaluating frontier models now have another xAI API option for coding, agentic tasks, and knowledge work, with a reported 500,000-token context window and listed pricing of $1.60 per million input tokens and $4.80 per million output tokens.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters offered mixed early impressions: some welcomed the faster release cadence and hoped Grok 5 would be a larger step forward, while others reported that Grok 4.7 felt slower, more token-intensive, or still below their practical threshold for coding and agentic workflows. Several remarks speculated about pricing, model size, release timing, benchmark competitiveness, and token usage, but these points were anecdotal or unverified in the supplied evidence.
