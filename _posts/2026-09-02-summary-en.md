### [Google announces Gemini 3.8 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google announced Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, with the supplied source pointing to a DeepMind model card for Gemini 3.8 Flash. The item is notable for AI and software engineering readers because Flash models are positioned around practical speed and cost-sensitive use, while the Cyber variant signals a security-focused specialization. The available source content here does not include detailed specifications, pricing, benchmark methodology, availability, or compatibility constraints, so concrete technical claims beyond the model names should be treated as limited. Hacker News discussion focused on early hands-on impressions around speed, HTML and JavaScript generation, multimodal analysis, benchmarks, and comparisons with Gemini 3.7 and other flagship models.

hackernews · bratao · Sep 2, 15:12 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49537553)

**「Background」** Gemini Flash is Google DeepMind’s faster model tier in the Gemini family, designed to balance quality, cost, and latency with adjustable effort levels. Google also publishes model cards for these models so users can see known limitations, mitigation notes, and safety evaluations. Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber is a specialized variant aimed at cybersecurity work, which Google says is offered to trusted defenders through the Fairwind Program.

**「Impact」** Developers and AI product teams have new Google Gemini variants to evaluate for fast coding, multimodal, and cybersecurity-related workflows, but should verify model-card details and real workload performance before relying on community benchmark impressions.

**「Community discussion」** Commenters were broadly interested in Gemini 3.8 Flash’s speed and apparent coding ability, with one example producing an HTML/JavaScript demo in 13 seconds for 1.8 cents and others citing strong third-party benchmark placements. Discussion also included caution that practical quality remains to be tested, a possible regression at low thinking effort versus Gemini 3.7, and continued praise for Gemini’s audio and video input support compared with image-only flagship models from some competitors.
