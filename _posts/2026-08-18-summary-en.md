### [Qwen 3.8 27B matches larger models](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/17/qwen-38-27b-scores-52/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Simon Willison reports that Qwen 3.8 27B scored 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. That matches GPT-5.6 Luna at its maximum score and is one point behind GLM-5.2 max and DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 max. The result is notable because Qwen 3.8 27B has 27B parameters, while GLM-5.2 is listed at 753B parameters and DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 at 1.7T parameters; GPT-5.6 Luna’s size is unknown. Willison characterizes Qwen 3.8 27B as an unusually strong model relative to its size.

rss · Simon Willison · Aug 17, 23:58

**「Background」** The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index is a third-party benchmark that aggregates model performance into a single comparative score, so it is useful for quick comparisons but should not be treated as a complete measure of model capability. Qwen is Alibaba’s open-weight large language model family, and parameter count, such as 27B, is a rough indicator of model size rather than a direct guarantee of quality or efficiency.

**「Impact」** If the benchmark result reflects real-world capability, Qwen 3.8 27B gives AI developers a much smaller open-weight model option that appears competitive with far larger systems on this index.
