A Telegram item citing Bloomberg says August 31 is Tim Cook’s final day as Apple CEO, with 51-year-old hardware executive John Ternus taking over on September 1 and Cook remaining as executive chairman. The report frames Ternus’s main task as accelerating Apple’s AI rollout, including addressing delays around the Siri upgrade. It also claims Apple will introduce its first foldable iPhone at a September 9 fall event, with 12 GB of RAM and deeper Siri AI integration able to use context from the screen, calendar, and camera to understand real-world situations. The item is brief and secondhand, so these claims should be treated as reported details rather than independently verified facts here.

**「Impact」** Apple developers and accessory makers would need to prepare for a September product cycle centered on AI-enabled Siri features and a new foldable iPhone form factor under John Ternus.
