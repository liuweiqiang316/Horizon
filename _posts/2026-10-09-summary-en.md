### [Cloudflare Acquires Deno](https://deno.com/blog/cloudflare) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Cloudflare has acquired Deno, the company behind the Deno JavaScript and TypeScript runtime. The supplied discussion quotes the announcement as saying Cloudflare will support the Deno runtime for one more year with monthly bug-fix and security releases, then end its own development of the runtime. Deno is expected to remain open source, with future development dependent on outside maintainers if the quoted plan is accurate. The acquisition matters because Deno has been a prominent alternative to Node.js and part of the broader edge-computing and JavaScript tooling ecosystem.

hackernews · ilreb · Oct 9, 13:03 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50019911)

**「Background」** Deno is a JavaScript and TypeScript runtime created by Ryan Dahl, who also created Node.js, and it has been positioned as a more secure, modern alternative with built-in tooling. Cloudflare operates Workers, an edge-computing platform with its own runtime, so this acquisition connects two JavaScript runtime and serverless-edge ecosystems.

**「Impact」** Developers relying on the Deno runtime appear to have a one-year maintenance window before Cloudflare stops active runtime development, unless another maintainer continues the project.

**「Community」** Hacker News commenters largely expressed disappointment and concern, with several describing the deal as an acquihire or the effective end of Deno runtime development. Some connected the news to wider consolidation of developer tooling companies by larger AI, cloud, and infrastructure firms.
